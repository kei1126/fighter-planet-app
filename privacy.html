In short

This game does not collect any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts. The game does not connect to the internet.

1. Information we collect

Army vs. Fighter Planet (“the app”) does not collect any information that can identify you, such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. The app does not send any data to an outside server.

2. Data stored only on your device

So that you can continue your game, the app saves the following only on your device. It is never sent to anyone, including the developer.

  • Missions cleared, scores, credits and aircraft levels
  • Your chosen aircraft, colors and weapons
  • Settings (language, sound, inverted controls, and so on)

This data is removed when you delete the app.

3. Ads, analytics and third parties

The app has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no sign-in to outside services. We do not share any data with third parties. There are no in-app purchases.

4. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. If the way data is handled changes in an important way, we will also say so in the app’s update notes.
